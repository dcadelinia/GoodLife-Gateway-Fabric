# Compliance & Governance Readiness

Platform architecture is designed for U.S. federal program participation and international recognition.  
All items below are designed into the current system. Remaining documentation can be completed during Phase 1–2 pilots.

## U.S. Federal
- Private-land focus only
- Ratepayer-protection modeling
- AI governance (human oversight, bias screening, audit logs) aligned with OMB / agency expectations
- Read-only, consent-based data principles
- Environmental trigger checklist (NEPA, CWA, CAA, ESA)
- USDA rural-development pathway compatibility
- Immutable audit trail of financial, spatial & decision data
- Explicit non-advisory posture

## International (EU Baseline)
- EU AI Act risk classification reviewed; transparency & human oversight in place
- GDPR-aligned controls (consent, purpose limitation, minimization, encryption)
- Energy & water efficiency metrics ready
- ESG / sustainability disclosure readiness
- Modular region-specific compliance modules
- Data-sovereignty & cross-border controls

## Operational Controls
- Validation Before Execution gate on every action
- Third-party / internal compliance review cadence
- Client materials clearly separate platform coordination from licensed advice
- Cybersecurity & resilience documentation for decision systems
